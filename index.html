The problem

Whatever happened on that pitch — the missed chance, the mistake that led to the goal, the bench time — players process it alone. No structure. No coaching. No support. That window is empty.

How it works

From situation to insight in four steps

A coach describes a player. The AI builds a module. The player completes it. The coach sees everything.

1
Coach describes the situation
Plain language. "Marcus goes silent after mistakes." No forms or menus. The AI understands coaching language.
2
AI generates a personalised module
A situation, four reflection questions, and a mental habit to practise — built for that specific player, position, and age group.
3
Player completes it independently
On their phone. 5 minutes. Then a live AI debrief challenges their answers and builds the mental habit.
4
Coach sees everything
Responses, AI insights, training flags, and completion rates. The 48-hour window becomes your most productive coaching time.
